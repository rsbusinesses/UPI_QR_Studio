UNDERSTANDING UPI

What Is Unified Payments Interface?

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is an instant payment system in India that enables eligible users to transfer money between participating bank accounts using supported applications.

Depending on the application and service, users can send money, pay merchants, and make payments by scanning a compatible QR code.

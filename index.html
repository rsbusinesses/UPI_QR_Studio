HOW IT WORKS

How to create and use your UPI QR code

This tool creates a QR code that can open a UPI payment screen in a compatible app. It does not collect or receive a payment.

Required: Enter the complete UPI ID exactly as shown in your own bank or payment app. Do not guess the provider handle or use an identifier copied from an unverified message.

Optional: Add a payee name, a positive amount in INR (up to two decimal places), and a payment note of up to 100 characters. Leave any of these fields blank when they are not needed.

  1. Check the UPI ID in the official app or account information provided by your bank or payment service.
  2. Enter the UPI ID and any optional details, then select Generate QR Code.
  3. Review the QR preview and the UPI ID, payee name and amount shown below it. Correct the form and generate it again if anything is wrong.
  4. Select Download QR Code to save a PNG image. The UPI ID, payee name and amount are displayed as labels in the image; the payment note is encoded in the QR data but is not printed as a separate label.
  5. Before displaying or sharing the image, scan it with a trusted UPI app and check the recipient details and amount shown by that app. A payer should authorize payment only when those details match the intended transaction.

What this generator does not do

The generator does not check whether a UPI ID exists, is active, belongs to you or can receive money. It does not connect to a bank, send or receive funds, confirm that a payment has succeeded, or provide a payment receipt. A generated or downloaded QR code is not proof that money has been received.

If the QR code does not scan

  • Check that the QR image is fully visible, sharply displayed or printed, and not cropped, covered or damaged.
  • Try a compatible, up-to-date UPI app with a working internet connection. The QR-generation library is bundled with this website, so it does not need a separate third-party library request.
  • Do not use or share the code if the app shows an unexpected recipient or amount. Recheck the UPI ID with its owner and generate a fresh code.

For a simpler QR containing only a UPI ID, see the standalone UPI QR generator. It does not include the optional payee name, amount or note fields available here.

Safety: Never enter or share your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or banking password in this website. A PIN is not needed merely to receive money.

UNDERSTANDING UPI

What Is Unified Payments Interface?

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is an instant payment system in India that enables eligible users to transfer money between participating bank accounts using supported applications.

Depending on the application and service, users can send money, pay merchants, and make payments by scanning a compatible QR code.

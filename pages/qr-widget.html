How to create and download your UPI QR code

  1. Open your bank or UPI app and copy the exact UPI ID you want the QR code to contain.
  2. Enter the part before the @ symbol in the first field and the provider handle after @ in the second field. The example shows the format username@provider.
  3. Select Generate QR Code. The widget joins the two parts with @, converts the result to lowercase and creates a QR preview.
  4. Check the generated UPI ID shown below the preview. Select Download QR Code to save a PNG containing the QR and the UPI ID label.
  5. Before sharing the image, scan it with a trusted UPI app and verify the recipient details displayed by that app. Do not authorize a payment just to test the code.

What this QR code contains

This widget creates a basic UPI payment URI using the payee-address parameter (pa) and the UPI ID you entered. It does not add a payee name, amount or payment note. Use the homepage QR generator when you need those optional fields.

What the format check can—and cannot—confirm

The widget checks only that the two parts contain the allowed characters. It does not check whether the UPI ID exists, is active, belongs to a person or business, is linked to an eligible account, or can receive payments. A valid-looking format is not proof that the address is correct.

Generating or downloading a QR code does not send money, verify a bank account, confirm that funds have arrived or process a payment. The payer must check the recipient and any transaction details shown in their own UPI app before authorizing a debit.

If the QR code does not scan

Check that the saved image is complete and clear, verify the UPI ID against the official app, and try a compatible UPI app. This page loads its QR-generation library from an external content-delivery service, so the library may not be available without a working connection.

Safety: Never enter or share your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or banking password in a QR generator. You do not need to disclose a UPI PIN to receive money.

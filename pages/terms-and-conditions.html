4.1 Details entered by you

You are responsible for the accuracy and legality of the information you enter into the QR generator, including any UPI ID, payee name, amount, or payment note that the tool supports.

4.2 Browser-based processing

The QR generator is intended to create QR codes within your browser. Under the current design, the details entered into the QR generator are processed locally and are not intentionally sent to the UPI QR Studio application server as part of QR generation. This statement must be updated if the technical implementation changes.

Your browser, hosting provider, analytics tools, advertising partners, or other third-party technologies may separately process technical information as described in our Privacy Policy.

4.3 Verify before sharing or paying

Always inspect the generated QR code and verify the recipient's UPI ID, displayed name, amount, and other payment details before sharing it or authorising a payment. Where possible, scan the QR code with a trusted UPI application and check the details displayed by that application before proceeding.

4.4 No payment confirmation

Creating, previewing, or downloading a QR code does not transfer money, confirm that a payment has been received, validate the recipient's bank account, or guarantee that a payment will succeed. Payment processing and confirmation occur through the relevant UPI application, bank, or payment service.

4.5 Downloaded files

If the website offers QR code downloads, you are responsible for checking the downloaded file and ensuring that it displays and scans correctly before printing, publishing, or distributing it.

THE ESSENTIALS

Remember these four rules

A few careful habits can help you avoid common digital payment scams.

01

Never share your PIN

Keep your UPI PIN, OTP, passwords and banking authentication details private.

02

Verify before paying

Check the displayed recipient and amount before authorizing a transaction.

03

Reject unexpected requests

Do not approve unfamiliar collect requests, mandates or payment instructions.

04

Act quickly if something happens

Contact your bank immediately if you notice unauthorized activity or suspect fraud.

URGENT ACTION

Think you've been scammed?

Contact your bank immediately and report suspected cyber financial fraud through India's official reporting channels. Fast reporting can help authorities and financial institutions respond.

The Indian National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal identifies 1930 as the helpline for immediate reporting of cyber financial fraud. Official portal.

1

Contact your bank

Use the official app or verified bank helpline to report the unauthorized transaction and ask about securing your account.

2

Report the financial fraud

Call 1930 promptly and follow the official process. Complete the online complaint process as instructed.

3

Preserve your evidence

Keep transaction references, bank alerts, relevant messages, phone numbers and complaint acknowledgement details.

4

KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

Common UPI scams and how to avoid them

Fraudsters may create urgency, impersonate trusted people or manipulate payment steps. Learn what to look for and what to do instead.

01
QR CODE FRAUD

Fake QR codes and refund scams

Someone claims that scanning a QR code and entering your PIN is necessary to receive a refund, prize or payment.

Protect yourself

Do not authorize a transaction to receive money. Scan only when you intend to pay and verify the details shown in your app.

02
FAKE PAYMENT PROOF

Fake screenshots and SMS alerts

A buyer or customer shows an edited screenshot or message claiming that a payment has been completed.

Protect yourself

Check your own UPI app and bank statement. Do not hand over goods based only on a screenshot or the other person's phone.

03
COLLECT REQUEST

Unexpected payment requests

A request appears in your app asking you to authorize a payment that you did not expect.

Protect yourself

Read the request carefully. Decline unfamiliar requests and verify with the person or business through a trusted channel.

04
IMPERSONATION

Fake bank or customer support

A caller pretends to be a bank employee, payment app representative, police officer or customer support agent.

Protect yourself

Never disclose your PIN or OTP. End suspicious calls and contact the organization using its official details.

05
REMOTE ACCESS

Screen-sharing and remote-control apps

A stranger asks you to install an app or share your screen to fix a payment, complete a refund or verify your account.

Protect yourself

Do not grant remote access to unknown people. If you already did, disconnect, remove the access and contact your bank if financial information may be exposed.

06
PHISHING

Fake links, apps and websites

A message asks you to update KYC, unblock an account, claim cashback or resolve a payment through an unfamiliar link.

Protect yourself

Avoid unexpected links and APK files. Open the official app or type the verified website address yourself.

07
SIM AND ACCOUNT TAKEOVER

SIM swapping and number misuse

Criminals attempt to take control of a mobile number or exploit access to your phone, messages or accounts.

Protect yourself

Contact your telecom provider if your SIM unexpectedly stops working. Secure your bank, email and phone accounts promptly.

08
SOCIAL ENGINEERING

Urgent threats and emotional pressure

A person pressures you to transfer money immediately, claims a family member is in trouble or threatens account suspension.

Protect yourself

Pause. Independently verify the person's identity and the situation before sending money or sharing information.

09
RECOVERY SCAM

Promises to recover stolen money

Someone contacts you after a fraud and promises guaranteed recovery in exchange for an advance fee or secret information.

Protect yourself

Do not pay recovery agents or disclose authentication details. Follow up only through your bank, police and official reporting channels.

10
FAKE INVESTMENT

Guaranteed returns and fake rewards

An offer promises guaranteed profits, cashback, lottery winnings or rewards in return for an advance payment.

Protect yourself

Independently verify the company and offer. Do not send money merely to unlock a supposed reward or profit.

11
MERCHANT FRAUD

Fake merchant QR replacement

A payment QR displayed at a shop or service location may have been replaced or altered by an unauthorized person.

Protect yourself

Verify the merchant name displayed in your payment app. Businesses should inspect their payment displays regularly.

12
MALICIOUS MANDATES

Unwanted AutoPay mandates

You are persuaded to approve a recurring payment mandate without understanding its merchant, amount or schedule.

Protect yourself

Read mandate details before approving. Review active mandates in your app and cancel unwanted ones through the supported official process.

YOUR SECRET STAYS SECRET

Understand your PIN, OTP and payment approval

Fraudsters often try to make a genuine payment appear to be a verification step. Understand what you are authorizing before entering a code.

✓

Enter a UPI PIN only in the genuine payment app when you intend to authorize the transaction shown.

PIN

UPI PIN

A secret code used to authorize applicable UPI transactions. Never tell it to a caller, merchant or supposed support agent.

OTP

One-time password

A verification code used in certain authentication processes. Never disclose it to an unknown person or enter it into a website reached through a suspicious link.

APP

App and screen-lock passwords

Use unique, hard-to-guess credentials. Do not reuse banking passwords across unrelated websites.

REQ

Payment requests and mandates

Read the amount, recipient, frequency and terms. Decline anything you did not initiate or cannot verify.

BEFORE YOU AUTHORIZE

Follow the STOP–CHECK–CONFIRM method

Build a simple verification routine before every unfamiliar or important payment.

S

Stop

Pause when a request creates urgency, fear or pressure. Do not rush because someone says your account will be blocked.

T

Take a closer look

Check the recipient, amount, request type, payment purpose and any recurring mandate details shown in your app.

O

Obtain independent confirmation

Contact the person or business through a known phone number or official channel if anything is unexpected.

P

Proceed only if verified

Authorize the payment only when you understand it and intend to make it. Otherwise, cancel or decline.

FOR SHOPKEEPERS AND BUSINESSES

Keep your business payments safe

Merchants and service providers can also be targeted through fake payment proof, QR replacement and social-engineering scams.

  • ✓ Verify incoming payments through your own bank or official merchant application.
  • ✓ Do not release goods or services solely because a customer displays a screenshot.
  • ✓ Inspect displayed QR codes regularly and replace damaged or suspicious displays.
  • ✓ Restrict access to the phone or device used to manage business payments.
  • ✓ Reconcile transaction records and receipts when reviewing daily sales.
  • ✓ Train staff to identify impersonation, unexpected refund requests and fake support calls.

PROTECT YOUR DIGITAL LIFE

Secure your phone, SIM and accounts

Payment safety also depends on protecting the device, mobile number and accounts used to access your financial services.

01

Protect your phone

  • Use a strong screen lock.
  • Install system updates promptly.
  • Download apps only from trusted sources.
  • Remove applications you no longer need.
02

Protect your SIM

  • Never share SIM verification codes.
  • Contact your operator if service unexpectedly disappears.
  • Use the operator's official process to secure a lost SIM.
  • Report a lost phone and secure relevant accounts quickly.
03

Protect your accounts

  • Use unique passwords where applicable.
  • Secure the email account used for banking alerts.
  • Review account alerts and transaction history.
  • Never approve authentication prompts you did not initiate.
COMMON QUESTIONS

UPI safety FAQs

Quick answers to common questions about secure payments and fraud prevention.

Do I need my UPI PIN to receive money?

You do not need to disclose your UPI PIN to another person to receive money. Never enter a PIN merely because someone says it is needed to receive a refund, prize or incoming payment.

Is it safe to scan any UPI QR code?

No QR code should be trusted automatically. Check the recipient and transaction details shown in your app. Proceed only if you intend to pay that recipient.

What if I shared an OTP or UPI PIN?

Contact your bank immediately if you believe the information could be used to access your account or authorize a transaction. Follow the bank's instructions to secure the account and report any unauthorized activity.

What should I do if a UPI payment is pending?

Check the status in your app and verify whether the amount was debited. Avoid repeating the payment while its outcome is uncertain. Follow the app or bank's stated resolution process.

Can I get money back after a UPI scam?

Recovery is not guaranteed. Report suspected fraud immediately to your bank and the official cyber financial fraud reporting channel. Provide accurate transaction details and preserve your complaint references.

Where can I report cyber financial fraud in India?

Call the national cyber financial fraud helpline at 1930 promptly and visit the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Also notify your bank immediately.

How can I check whether a payment was received?

Check your own UPI app transaction history and bank account records. Do not rely solely on screenshots, messages or the payer's device.

Is a caller from my bank allowed to ask for my UPI PIN?

Never disclose your UPI PIN to a caller or supposed support agent. End the call and contact the bank through a verified official channel if you are uncertain.

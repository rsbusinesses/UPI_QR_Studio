WHO WE ARE

A simpler way to explore UPI.

Digital payments have become part of everyday life. However, people may still need clear instructions for creating QR codes, understanding UPI IDs, exploring payment apps, and learning how to avoid common scams.

UPI QR Studio brings these topics together in one convenient place. Our aim is to make useful information easier to understand, while helping visitors navigate digital payment tools with greater confidence.

OUR PURPOSE

What guides our work

We focus on usefulness, clarity, and responsible digital payment awareness.

OUR MISSION

Make UPI easier to understand.

To provide straightforward tools and educational content that help users understand UPI QR codes, payment IDs, provider options, and essential payment safety practices.

OUR VISION

Build confidence in digital payments.

To make digital payment information more accessible and understandable, so that people can make informed decisions when using available UPI tools and services.

SAFETY & TRANSPARENCY

Helpful information. Responsible use.

UPI QR Studio is an informational and utility website. We encourage users to protect their UPI PIN and other confidential credentials, check recipient details before authorising payments, and rely on official bank and payment provider channels for account-specific assistance.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, our website should not be understood as a bank, payment service provider, or an official representative of NPCI, RBI, or any listed payment app. Provider names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

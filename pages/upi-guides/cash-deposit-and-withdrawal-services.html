UPI Cash Deposit and Withdrawal Services

What Is UPI Cash Point?

NPCI describes UPI Cash Point as a cash-withdrawal service at participating, authorised Business Correspondent (BC) touchpoints. It is not available at every shop that accepts ordinary UPI payments. This named service is for cash withdrawal; do not assume it is also a cash-deposit facility. Confirm that the outlet and your bank or payment app support the specific service.

Before you begin

  • Use the UPI app supported by your bank or service provider and confirm that your account is eligible for the cash-withdrawal service.
  • Ask the BC outlet to confirm that it is currently offering UPI Cash Point withdrawals and explain the amount, any applicable fee and receipt process.
  • Keep control of your own phone. Enter authentication only in your UPI app and never tell the operator your PIN or OTP.

How a withdrawal works

  1. Visit an authorised BC touchpoint and request a cash withdrawal.
  2. Ask the BC to display its UPI Cash Point QR code, then scan it using your UPI app.
  3. Check the service or recipient details and amount displayed in your app. Cancel if they are unexpected or do not match the withdrawal you requested.
  4. Authorize the debit only when the details are correct and you intend to withdraw that amount.
  5. Wait for the final successful status. The BC should then hand over the cash; count it and collect the receipt before leaving.

What to check afterwards

Check the debit and transaction reference in your own UPI app or bank record, and keep the receipt. If your account is debited but no cash—or the wrong amount—is handed over, do not immediately retry. Contact your bank or payment provider and the outlet through verified channels, quoting the transaction reference.

A QR code on a shop counter does not by itself prove that the outlet is an authorised cash point. Confirm the service and outlet before authorizing a withdrawal.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming that every UPI-accepting shop also provides cash withdrawal.
  • Confusing an ordinary merchant payment QR with a UPI Cash Point withdrawal QR.
  • Authorizing a debit before checking the outlet, service, amount and any displayed charge.
  • Leaving without counting the cash, collecting a receipt or checking the transaction status.

How to Withdraw Cash Using UPI

A UPI-linked cash withdrawal requires a supported bank or service-provider flow and a participating point that can dispense cash. Before confirming, check the amount, displayed recipient or operator details, any charges and how the transaction receipt will be issued.

How Cash Withdrawal at a Participating Shop Works

At a participating shop, cash withdrawal is available only when the operator is enabled for that service and the transaction follows the official process. Verify the outlet and amount, wait for the app’s final status, and count the cash before leaving.

How to Deposit Cash Through Supported Services

Cash deposit through a UPI-enabled or linked service is not available at every bank, device or location. Confirm that the particular point accepts deposits, understand how the cash is counted and receipted, and review any amount or eligibility limits before proceeding.

Do You Need a Debit Card for QR-Based Cash Services?

A debit card may or may not be required depending on the participating service, bank and authentication flow. Do not infer eligibility from the presence of a QR code; check the provider’s current instructions for the specific cash service.

What Are the Cash Withdrawal Limits?

Cash withdrawal caps can depend on the bank, service network, transaction method and the limits configured for the account or outlet. Check the amount displayed by the official provider and distinguish a per-transaction cap from any daily or account-level cap.

Are Charges Applicable to Cash Services?

Charges depend on the provider, service type, account terms and amount. Review the displayed fee or official tariff before approving the transaction, and keep the receipt in case the amount debited differs from the amount dispensed.

How to Find a Participating Cash Point

Use the relevant bank or service provider’s official locator or support channel to confirm the address and the exact service offered. Availability can change, so do not rely solely on an old directory entry, signboard or third-party listing.

What to Do If Your Account Is Debited but You Receive No Cash

If a debit appears but the cash was not dispensed, do not immediately repeat the withdrawal. Save the transaction reference, time, amount and outlet details; check the app and bank record, then contact the bank or service provider through its official dispute process.

How Businesses Can Become Cash Service Points

A business interested in offering cash services should apply through an authorized bank or service-provider channel. Before onboarding, review eligibility, settlement, staff training, cash handling, receipts, security duties and any contractual or regulatory conditions.

Practical checks before you act

Service availability depends on the bank, cash point, participating network and local rules.

  • Confirm the outlet is an authorized service point.
  • Review fees, transaction limits and receipt details before confirming.
  • Keep the receipt and verify the credit or debit in the official app or statement.

NPCI's UPI Cash Point page describes cash withdrawal at authorized Business Correspondent touchpoints and lists a ₹5,000 per-transaction limit for that service, subject to other applicable regulatory limits. This is not a universal limit for every cash-in or cash-out product. Keep the receipt, count the cash before leaving and contact the authorized operator or bank if the recorded amount and cash received differ.

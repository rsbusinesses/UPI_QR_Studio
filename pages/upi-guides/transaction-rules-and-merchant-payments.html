UPI Transaction Rules and Merchant Payments

How to Send Money Using UPI

A UPI payment can be started using a UPI ID, a supported mobile-number or contact flow, a QR code, or another option offered by your app. The method may differ, but you should always verify the resolved recipient and amount before authorizing a debit.

Before you begin

  • Use the official UPI app from your bank or chosen payment provider and select the bank account you intend to pay from.
  • Get the recipient's exact UPI ID, verified contact details or QR code from a trusted source.
  • Check the applicable transaction limit and any fee displayed by your bank or app; limits and charges are not necessarily the same for every payment type.

Steps to send a payment

  1. Open your UPI app and choose the transfer method you intend to use.
  2. Enter or scan the recipient details and wait for the app to resolve the recipient.
  3. Compare the displayed recipient name with the person or business you intend to pay. Cancel if it is missing, unexpected or does not match.
  4. Enter the amount and any reference or note requested by the app. Review the recipient, amount and payment purpose once more.
  5. Authorize the debit only in the official app and only when you intend to make that payment. Wait for the final status and save the transaction reference.

What to check afterwards

Check the transaction status in your app and, where needed, your bank account record. If the transaction is pending or the outcome is unclear, do not send it again until you have checked whether the first payment completed. If money was debited but the recipient did not receive it, retain the reference and use the official complaint process in your app or bank.

A QR scan, payment screenshot or SMS is not proof that a payment has settled. Check the transaction in your own app or receiving account.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming the UPI ID or QR code proves the identity of the intended recipient.
  • Approving a collect request or mandate that you did not expect; approval may authorize money to leave your account.
  • Entering your UPI PIN before checking who will receive the money and how much will be debited.
  • Repeating a pending payment without first checking its status and bank record.

How to Receive Money Through UPI

To receive money, share a payment address or QR code that you control and confirm receipt in your own app or bank record. You do not need to disclose a UPI PIN or approve an unexpected debit request simply to receive funds.

How to Scan and Pay Using a QR Code

A payment QR can prefill a UPI address and sometimes an amount or transaction note. Scanning it does not prove the recipient is trustworthy: inspect the name and amount shown in your own payment app before authorizing the debit.

How to Pay Online Using UPI

An online UPI payment may open your chosen payment app from a merchant checkout page. Check that the merchant, amount and order details match the purchase and complete authorization only in the trusted payment interface.

How to Pay at a Shop Using UPI

When paying at a shop, compare the payee or merchant name and amount on your payment screen with the shop’s displayed bill. Keep the transaction result and merchant receipt if available, particularly when the app shows a pending status.

How to Pay Using a UPI ID

When entering a UPI ID manually, type or paste it carefully and inspect the recipient name resolved by the payment app. An ID is a routing address, not proof of identity, so cancel if the displayed payee does not match your intended recipient.

How to Use a Collect Payment Request

A collect request asks you to review and potentially approve a payment; accepting it may authorize money to leave your account. Approve only a request you expected and understand, and never treat a request to pay as a requirement for receiving money.

Can You Use UPI 24 Hours a Day?

UPI services are designed for frequent, round-the-clock use, but a transaction can still be affected by maintenance, bank downtime, app outages, network problems or risk controls. Check the status shown by your bank and payment app when a service is unavailable.

What Is the UPI Transaction Limit?

There is no single limit that should be assumed for every UPI transaction. Applicable caps can vary by bank, transaction category, account history and provider rules; check current official instructions for the specific payment you intend to make.

Are There Any Charges for UPI Payments?

Fees can depend on the transaction type, merchant arrangement, bank terms and applicable rules. Check the current fee disclosure in the app or from the provider for your specific transaction instead of assuming that every payment has the same charge.

How to Check Whether a Payment Was Successful

Use the transaction status inside your payment app and, when necessary, compare it with your bank account record. Keep the transaction ID for follow-up and do not rely on a screenshot, SMS alone or the other party’s claim as proof of settlement.

How to Download or Share Your UPI QR Code

Before sharing or printing a payment QR, inspect the encoded payment details and test the code using a trusted app where appropriate. A QR may expose the UPI ID and may prefill an amount or note, so share it only where intended and keep private information out of the note.

How Merchants Can Accept UPI Payments

A merchant can explore QR or other UPI acceptance through its bank or an authorized payment provider. Before displaying a code, confirm that the settlement account is correct, understand reconciliation and refund processes, and train staff to verify payment status from the merchant’s own records.

What Is a Merchant Category Code?

A merchant category code (MCC) identifies the type of business associated with a merchant account. Use the category provided through the official acquiring or payment provider, because misclassification can affect payment handling or eligibility under some provider rules.

How Merchant Refunds Work

A merchant refund follows the merchant’s and payment provider’s refund workflow and may take time to appear in the customer’s account. Keep the original transaction ID and refund reference, and contact the merchant and bank through official channels if the expected timeframe passes.

How to Pay for an IPO Through UPI

An IPO application may use a supported UPI mandate or approval flow, subject to the broker, bank and offer instructions. Verify the application details, amount and mandate request through the official platform and observe the published deadline; do not approve unrelated requests.

Practical checks before you act

Payment status and limits can depend on the bank, payment type, risk controls and service availability.

  • Check the status inside the app before retrying a pending payment.
  • For a debit without recipient credit, follow the bank/app dispute process.
  • Never treat a screenshot or SMS alone as proof of settlement.

For payment rules and category-specific limits, consult the NPCI UPI FAQs and the current instructions from your bank. RBI has permitted NPCI to prescribe and revise limits for Person-to-Merchant payments in consultation with banks, while the P2P framework is distinct; do not assume one number applies to every payment type. See the RBI publication on payment-system developments. Preserve the order details, status and transaction reference for a dispute.

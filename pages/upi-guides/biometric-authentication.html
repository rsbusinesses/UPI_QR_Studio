Biometric Authentication for UPI

What Is Biometric Authentication?

Biometric authentication checks a device-supported characteristic, such as a fingerprint or face, to help confirm that the device user is present. In a payment app, it may unlock access or support a verification step; it does not automatically replace the UPI PIN for every transaction.

Before you begin

  • Use an updated, official UPI application and a secure device.
  • Keep your registered mobile number active when the service requires mobile verification.
  • Check the feature’s eligibility, transaction limits and any displayed charges before proceeding.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Update the operating system and the official UPI app, then open the app yourself.
  2. Look in the app’s security, profile or payment settings for the biometric feature; do not assume it is available on every account.
  3. Follow the on-screen eligibility checks and consent prompts. Use only the official device or app interface.
  4. If verification fails, clean the sensor or camera, improve lighting, check device permissions and retry only as directed.
  5. Use the alternative method explicitly offered by the app or bank, such as the UPI PIN or another approved verification process. If the feature remains unavailable, contact official support.

What to check afterwards

Return to the app’s transaction history or feature settings and confirm the final status. Keep the transaction reference, date, amount and any receipt if you need to follow up. If a debit, credit or permission does not match what you expected, use the in-app help option and contact your bank through an official channel.

Biometric verification does not mean you should tell anyone your PIN or OTP. Do not install an app or share your screen at the request of an unknown caller claiming to fix authentication.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Relying only on a screenshot, SMS or another person’s claim instead of checking your own app or account.
  • Approving a request without reading the amount, recipient and action being authorized.
  • Assuming all banks, apps, devices and accounts support the same features or limits.
  • Sharing confidential authentication details or following unexpected instructions from callers.

How Fingerprint Authentication Works for UPI

Fingerprint verification typically uses the phone’s enrolled biometric system when the app and device support it. The exact role of a fingerprint—app sign-in, a local confirmation or a payment-related check—depends on the provider’s implementation and the transaction being made.

How Face Authentication Works for UPI

Face verification can mean unlocking the phone, signing in to an app, or completing a separate identity check. Those are different purposes, so read the app’s prompt and do not assume that ordinary phone face unlock authorizes every UPI payment.

What Is Aadhaar Face Authentication?

Aadhaar face authentication is an identity-verification process, not simply the face-unlock feature on a phone. It is available only in supported flows and subject to the relevant identity service, bank, device and app requirements.

How to Set or Reset a UPI PIN Using Aadhaar Face Authentication

An Aadhaar-based UPI PIN setup or reset is available only where the bank and app support that method and the user meets the stated eligibility requirements. Follow the official in-app process and carefully distinguish identity verification from the final PIN-setting step.

What Is the Difference Between Phone Face Unlock and Aadhaar Face Authentication?

Phone face unlock generally controls access to a device; Aadhaar face authentication is used for a supported identity-verification workflow. One is not a substitute for the other, and the options shown by a bank or payment app determine what can be used for a particular task.

Which Banks and Apps Support Biometric UPI Features?

Biometric support can differ by bank, app version, device model and transaction type. Check the current feature notes published by your bank or payment app rather than assuming that a feature offered by one provider is available everywhere.

What to Do If Biometric Verification Fails

A failed biometric check can be caused by sensor or camera conditions, device settings, app compatibility or service-side eligibility. Use the troubleshooting options provided by the app, and switch to another method only when the official flow offers it.

Can You Use a UPI PIN Instead of Biometric Authentication?

Whether a UPI PIN can be used instead of a biometric step depends on the exact app flow and why the biometric check was requested. Follow the alternatives displayed by the bank or app; never share a PIN or OTP with a person offering to help.

Practical checks before you act

Biometrics are a device-level convenience, not a reason to share credentials.

  • Keep your device OS and payment app updated.
  • Never disclose the UPI PIN or approve a prompt you did not initiate.
  • Check the app and bank instructions because supported authentication methods differ.

NPCI's October 2025 circular on additional UPI authentication methods describes optional UIDAI face authentication for PIN setup/reset and optional on-device biometrics for transaction authentication. Check current NPCI UPI circulars and your bank/app for later changes, availability and applicable limits.

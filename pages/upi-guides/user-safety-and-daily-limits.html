How to Keep Your UPI Account Safe

Account safety depends on securing both the device and the payment account. Use official apps, keep the phone updated and locked, verify payee details before payment, and report unexpected activity through your bank’s official channel.

Before you begin

  • For how to keep your upi account safe, use the official app provided by the relevant bank or service and keep your device protected with a screen lock and current security updates.
  • If how to keep your upi account safe requires mobile or device verification, keep the SIM registered with your bank available and follow the prompts in the official app.
  • Before using how to keep your upi account safe, confirm availability for your account and review the current limit and any fee shown by the provider.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open the official UPI app directly; do not use an unexpected link or a screen shared by a caller.
  2. Read the full request, including the recipient or merchant name, amount, purpose and whether money will be debited.
  3. Stop if the request is unexpected, urgent, asks for your PIN/OTP, or promises a refund or prize in exchange for approving a payment.
  4. Decline or cancel anything you cannot confidently verify. Contact the bank or app using contact details shown inside its official app.
  5. If you suspect misuse, secure your phone and account, contact your bank promptly, report the transaction through the app, and preserve the transaction ID and messages.

What to check afterwards

After how to keep your upi account safe, verify the outcome in your official payment app or bank records. Keep the transaction reference for follow-up, and do not repeat an unclear transaction until you have checked its status.

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, screen-lock code, banking password, or remote-access control. Enter a PIN only in the trusted payment app when you intend to authorize a debit.

How to Keep Your UPI Account Safe

Protecting a UPI account means protecting the device, registered mobile number and authentication details as well as checking each payment request. Use official apps and support channels, and treat unexpected requests for money or credentials as warning signs.

Before using a UPI app

  • Install the app from the bank's or payment provider's official website or an authorized app store.
  • Keep your phone protected with a screen lock and current operating-system and app updates.
  • Know which bank account and registered mobile number are linked to the app. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, banking password or screen-lock code.

Checks before authorizing a payment

  1. Open the app yourself rather than following an unsolicited payment or support link.
  2. Read the payee or merchant name, amount, payment purpose and action requested by the screen.
  3. Stop if a caller or message says you must enter a PIN to receive money, obtain a refund, claim a prize or unlock an account.
  4. Confirm unexpected requests independently using a known phone number or official support channel.
  5. Authorize only a payment you recognize and intend to make. Check the final status afterwards.

What to do if something looks wrong

If you notice an unauthorized debit or suspect that your phone, SIM or payment credentials have been compromised, contact your bank and payment provider promptly through official channels. Preserve the transaction reference, date, amount, alerts and relevant messages. In India, suspected cyber financial fraud can also be reported through the national helpline 1930 and the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Do not rely on a screenshot or a message from another person as proof that money was received. Check the receiving account's own transaction history or bank record.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Sharing a PIN, OTP or screen with someone claiming to be bank or app support.
  • Approving unfamiliar collect requests, mandates or payment prompts under pressure.
  • Installing remote-access software at the direction of an unknown caller.
  • Assuming one daily or per-transaction limit applies to every bank, account and payment category.

Why You Should Never Share Your UPI PIN

A UPI PIN is an authentication secret used to authorize a debit. Enter it only in the trusted payment app when you intend to pay; never disclose it to callers, merchants, support agents or anyone promising to receive money or issue a refund.

Why You Should Never Share Your OTP

An OTP is meant to confirm a specific login, registration or other action, depending on the service. Do not read it to another person or enter it after an unexpected prompt; check the message purpose and proceed only in the official flow you initiated.

How to Identify Fake Payment Requests

A suspicious request may arrive unexpectedly, use pressure or claim that approval is needed to receive a refund, prize or payment. Read whether the prompt asks you to send money, and decline anything you did not initiate or cannot verify.

How to Avoid Fraudulent QR Codes

A QR code can direct your app to a payee or prefill transaction details, but scanning does not validate the person or merchant behind it. Check the destination and amount in your own app and cancel if the resolved name differs from what you expect.

Do You Need a UPI PIN to Receive Money?

Receiving money into a UPI account normally does not require you to tell another person your UPI PIN. A prompt requesting a PIN usually relates to authorizing a debit, so read the transaction screen carefully and reject unexpected payment requests.

How to Identify Fake Payment Screenshots

A screenshot can be edited, delayed or unrelated to the transaction being discussed. For merchants and recipients, confirm the credit using the receiving account’s own transaction history or bank record before releasing goods or marking an order paid.

What to Do If Your Phone Is Lost

If the phone used for UPI is lost, contact your mobile operator and bank or payment provider through official channels as soon as practical. Secure or remotely lock the device where possible, ask about blocking payment access and monitor account activity.

What to Do If Your SIM Is Misused

Unexpected loss of mobile service, replacement-SIM messages or unrequested verification can indicate a mobile-account issue. Contact the operator using its official support route, alert your bank if payment access may be exposed and monitor transaction alerts.

What Is the Daily UPI Payment Limit?

A daily limit depends on the bank, account, app, transaction category and current rules. Use the amount stated by your provider for the intended payment type; avoid relying on a single figure found online as a universal cap.

Why Do UPI Transaction Limits Differ?

Limits may differ because a payment is classified by purpose, merchant type, risk setting, account type or provider-specific implementation. Check the rule for the exact transaction category with your bank or official payment app.

What Happens If You Enter the Wrong UPI PIN Repeatedly?

Repeated incorrect PIN attempts may cause a temporary block or require a reset under the bank’s security rules. Stop guessing, follow the official recovery steps shown by the app or contact the bank if you are unsure whether the account is locked.

How to Recognize Suspicious UPI Transactions

Suspicious activity can include a debit you did not authorize, an unfamiliar beneficiary, unexpected collect requests or notifications that do not match your actions. Check the account record directly and report discrepancies promptly through the bank or app.

How to Report UPI Fraud

If you suspect UPI fraud, promptly report it to your bank or payment app and preserve transaction IDs, timestamps, messages and other relevant records. Use current official government cybercrime-reporting resources where appropriate; avoid contact numbers or links sent by an unknown caller.

What to Do If Someone Makes an Unauthorized Payment

For an unauthorized debit, contact the bank or payment provider promptly using its official in-app or published channel, request the transaction be investigated and follow its dispute process. Preserve the transaction reference and do not share passwords, PINs or OTPs during follow-up.

Practical checks before you act

UPI limits are not one universal number for every user and transaction; check the applicable bank and app rules.

  • Verify the current cap in official bank/app information.
  • Different transaction categories may have different limits.
  • If a transfer fails, read the exact error and avoid repeated attempts without checking status.

See NPCI Fraud Awareness for payment-scam guidance. If you suspect cyber financial fraud in India, contact your bank/payment provider promptly and use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or its 1930 helpline. Preserve transaction references and status messages.

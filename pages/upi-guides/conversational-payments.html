Conversational Payments with UPI

What Is Hello! UPI?

Hello! UPI and related conversational payment services let eligible users access selected UPI functions through supported voice or assisted interfaces. This guide also covers UPI 123PAY, which provides feature-phone and other assisted payment journeys. These services are not available in the same way on every device, app, language or bank account; check the current instructions for the particular service you intend to use.

Before you begin

  • Confirm that your bank or payment provider supports the conversational or feature-phone service and your account is eligible.
  • Find the service entry point and phone number, if applicable, from the provider's official website or app. Do not rely on an unsolicited message or caller.
  • Choose a language you understand and use a private setting where you can hear and verify the payment details clearly.

How to make a voice-assisted payment

  1. Start from the official service channel published by your bank or supported provider.
  2. Select the language and payment action offered by that service, then follow the spoken or keypad prompts.
  3. Provide the recipient details and amount only through the official interface. Listen to or read the system's confirmation of the payee and amount.
  4. Stop and cancel if the recipient, amount or requested action is unclear or incorrect.
  5. Complete only the authentication requested by the official service, then wait for its final transaction status.

What to check afterwards

Check the transaction history and bank record for the final status and keep the reference number. A spoken prompt or voice confirmation is not, by itself, proof that the payment settled. If the result is pending or unclear, check the status before trying again.

Never disclose a UPI PIN or OTP to a caller or another person. Use authentication only within the secure flow you deliberately started through the official service.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Calling a payment number supplied by an unknown message or caller instead of confirming it with the provider.
  • Proceeding when the spoken payee name, amount or payment action cannot be understood.
  • Treating a voice prompt, call ending or message as proof of successful settlement without checking the account record.
  • Repeating a payment while the first transaction is still pending or its outcome is uncertain.

What Is UPI 123PAY?

UPI 123PAY is an initiative intended to make UPI payments possible through supported feature-phone and assisted access methods. Available channels and transaction options can change; confirm the current process through NPCI or the participating bank before using a particular number, IVR or device flow.

How to Make UPI Payments Using a Feature Phone

A feature-phone UPI payment uses a supported service flow rather than the full smartphone interface. Follow the instructions supplied by the participating provider, keep authentication details private and verify the payee and amount using the confirmation method available to that service.

How to Make Payments Using Voice Commands

Voice-assisted payments may collect details such as payee and amount through spoken prompts, but the available functionality depends on the service and language. Listen to the read-back carefully and do not continue if the payee, amount or action is unclear.

How Voice-Based Payment Confirmation Works

A voice confirmation is useful only if it clearly identifies the intended transaction. Listen for the payee, amount and requested action, and check any screen or receipt the service provides; a spoken prompt alone should not be treated as proof of final settlement.

Which Languages Are Supported?

Language support is defined by the individual service, access channel and provider, and may not be identical across all users. Check the official instructions for the languages currently offered and stop if you cannot confidently understand the payee, amount or confirmation prompt.

How to Verify Payment Details Before Confirming

Before confirming a voice- or conversation-led payment, verify the payee identity, amount, purpose and whether the action will debit money. Ask the interface to repeat details where supported, and cancel rather than guess when the information is incomplete.

What to Do If a Voice Payment Fails

If a voice-based transaction fails or its outcome is uncertain, first check the service status, transaction history and bank account. Keep the reference number or recorded error and use the official support route; avoid making a second payment until you know whether the first one completed.

Which Devices and Applications Support Conversational Payments?

Compatibility depends on the specific service, the device and network available, bank participation, account eligibility and the current app or IVR flow. Use provider-published compatibility information and never install unofficial software to gain access to a payment feature.

Practical checks before you act

Voice or chat interfaces can make payment flows easier, but they do not remove the need to verify the payee.

  • Review the displayed recipient and amount before authorization.
  • Do not follow payment instructions from an unsolicited caller or message.
  • Use the official app interface for confirmation and dispute reporting.

For feature-phone and voice-assisted services, follow the instructions of the specific official service and verify the payee and amount before authorizing a transaction. See the NPCI UPI FAQs; availability and supported languages or journeys can vary by bank, app and device.

UPI AutoPay and Recurring Payments

What Is UPI AutoPay?

UPI AutoPay supports eligible recurring payments through a mandate that defines the merchant or purpose and the terms of future debits. The supported frequency, amount thresholds and management actions depend on the provider and mandate conditions shown to the user.

  5. After what is upi autopay?, check the final status in the app or bank record and keep the transaction reference if you need to follow up.

What Is a UPI Mandate?

A UPI mandate records permission for a future or recurring payment under stated conditions. Before approving one, check the payee, maximum or fixed amount, schedule, validity period and the options available for viewing or revoking it.

Before you begin

  • For what is a upi mandate?, use the official app provided by the relevant bank or service and keep your device protected with a screen lock and current security updates.
  • If what is a upi mandate? requires mobile or device verification, keep the SIM registered with your bank available and follow the prompts in the official app.
  • Before using what is a upi mandate?, confirm availability for your account and review the current limit and any fee shown by the provider.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open the UPI app where the mandate was created and find AutoPay, Mandates, Recurring Payments or a similarly named section.
  2. Select the relevant merchant or mandate and review its status, amount or maximum amount, frequency, start date, end date and next debit date.
  3. Choose the available action, such as view, pause, resume, modify or cancel. Menu names and available actions vary by app and mandate.
  4. Read the confirmation screen carefully and complete any authentication requested by the app. Save the confirmation or reference number.
  5. Check the mandate status again and review your transaction history around the scheduled date. Contact the merchant and your bank if a debit does not match the mandate details.

What to check afterwards

After what is a upi mandate?, verify the outcome in your official payment app or bank records. Keep the transaction reference for follow-up, and do not repeat an unclear transaction until you have checked its status.

Cancelling a UPI mandate may stop future UPI debits but may not automatically cancel the underlying subscription or service contract. Check the merchant account as well.

Practical checks before you act

An AutoPay mandate is an authorization for future debits; review the mandate scope and schedule before approving it.

  • Check merchant name, maximum amount, frequency and validity.
  • Review active mandates periodically in the app or bank channel.

What Is UPI Circle?

UPI Circle is a delegated-payment arrangement in which a primary user gives a trusted secondary user permission to make eligible payments from the primary user's UPI account under defined controls. NPCI's overview says the secondary user may or may not have a bank account linked to UPI. Available roles and controls depend on the participating app and bank.

Before setting up delegation

  • Use an official UPI app that offers UPI Circle and confirm that the intended users are eligible.
  • Agree who will be the primary user and who will be the secondary user. Verify the invitation independently before accepting it.
  • Understand whether the arrangement uses individual transaction approval or another supported delegation mode, and check the spending limit, duration, notifications and removal process.

How to set up and manage a Circle

  1. The primary user opens UPI Circle in the supported app and starts an invitation using the official flow.
  2. The invited secondary user checks who sent the invitation and reads the permissions before accepting it in their own supported app.
  3. Review the payment mode, spending controls, expiry and approval requirements shown by the app. Set only the permissions that are needed.
  4. Both users should understand how transactions and notifications will appear and how to raise a concern.
  5. Review activity and remove or revoke delegated access through the official app when it is no longer required or trusted.

What to check afterwards

Confirm that the intended primary and secondary users, permissions and limits are shown in the app. Monitor delegated activity and transaction notifications. If a phone is lost or access is no longer appropriate, use the official removal process and contact the bank or app promptly if misuse is suspected.

Rules for UPI Circle on IoT devices or software profiles are a separate extension with their own scope and limits. Do not apply IoT-specific limits to every person-to-person delegation arrangement; check the terms for the exact feature in your app.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Sharing a UPI PIN or banking password instead of using the official delegation feature.
  • Accepting an unexpected invitation or granting access before verifying the other user.
  • Setting a limit or duration without understanding the permitted payment mode and approval requirements.
  • Assuming that controls for a person-to-person Circle are identical to those for an IoT device or software profile.

Who Is the Primary User?

The primary user is the person who sets up or controls a supported delegation and chooses the permissions available to the secondary user. They should understand the spending controls, notification settings, liability and removal options before granting access.

Who Is the Secondary User?

The secondary user receives delegated access under the permissions established for that arrangement. They should review the invitation, spending limit, approval requirements and duration, and accept only if they understand what actions they are authorized to take.

How to Add a Secondary User

Adding a secondary user should be performed inside the official app that offers the feature. Check the identity details, relationship, roles and requested permissions on both sides, and never approve an invitation received through an unrelated link or caller.

How to Accept a UPI Circle Invitation

Before accepting an invitation, verify who initiated it and read the payment permissions that will be granted. Accept only through the official app after confirming the identity, limit and approval conditions shown by the service.

Can Someone Use UPI Circle Without Their Own Linked Bank Account?

Whether a secondary user needs a separately linked bank account depends on the current implementation and role offered by the participating provider. Check the eligibility notes in the official app rather than relying on a general statement that applies to all providers.

How to Set Payment Limits for a Secondary User

Set the lowest practical spending limit and use any per-transaction or approval controls the service provides. The available options and maximum values can vary, so review them in the app and explain the arrangement clearly to the secondary user.

How to Approve or Manage Delegated Payments

The primary user should review incoming approval requests or delegated-payment activity in the app that manages the arrangement. Verify the payee, amount and purpose and change permissions if the observed usage no longer matches the agreed arrangement.

How to Check UPI Circle Transactions

Review delegated activity in the history or statements made available by the payment app or bank. Distinguish a request, a pending transfer and a completed debit, and retain transaction references if either user needs to query an entry.

How to Remove a Secondary User

Use the official management flow to revoke or remove delegated access and then confirm that the arrangement’s status has changed. If a device is lost or you suspect misuse, contact the bank or app promptly and review recent activity.

How to Keep Delegated Payments Safe

Delegation works best when both users understand the scope and agree on sensible limits. Review activity, keep authentication credentials private, and remove access when it is no longer needed or either user’s circumstances change.

Practical checks before you act

Delegated payment features have eligibility, spending controls and responsibilities defined by the participating app and bank.

  • Review the primary and secondary user roles before setup.
  • Set only an appropriate limit and monitor notifications.
  • Confirm current eligibility and controls in official app documentation.

NPCI describes UPI Circle as a feature through which a primary user can authorize a secondary user to make payments from the primary user's UPI account under defined controls. The secondary user may or may not have a bank account linked to UPI. Do not apply limits for IoT device profiles to every person-to-person delegation arrangement; review the terms and controls shown in the supported app.

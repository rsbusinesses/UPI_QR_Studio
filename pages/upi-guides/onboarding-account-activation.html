What You Need Before Using UPI

Before starting your UPI onboarding, ensure you have the following essential prerequisites ready:

  • Active Bank Account: An account with any NPCI-partnered bank in India.
  • Registered Mobile Number: The phone number linked to your bank account must be present in the device you are configuring.
  • Working registered SIM: Keep the mobile number registered with your bank available in the device you are setting up.
  • SMS and network access: Some app registration flows send an SMS to verify the mobile number or device. Your mobile operator may charge for an outgoing SMS; follow the app's current instructions.
  • Compatible device and official app: Use an up-to-date app from the bank or payment provider and a supported device.
  • Supported authentication method: Follow the method actually offered by your bank and app, which may include debit-card or Aadhaar-based verification. Availability varies.

How to Register for UPI

Registration establishes your identity on the Unified Payments Interface ecosystem. Follow these core steps:

  1. Install an Authorized Application: Download a certified UPI application (such as BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or your bank’s official mobile application) exclusively from official application stores.
  2. Language and Terms Selection: Launch the app, select your preferred operating language, and review the terms of service.
  3. Review permissions: Grant only the permissions the official app requests for a stated feature and that you understand. Requirements vary by app and device; do not grant unrelated permissions or remote access at the direction of a caller.

How to Verify Your Mobile Number

Depending on the app and bank, setup may include verifying the mobile number and binding the device to the registered SIM. The exact SMS and network steps are determined by the participating app, bank and mobile operator.

  1. Check the registered number: Confirm that the SIM in the phone matches the mobile number registered with your bank.
  2. Follow the app prompt: If the official app asks to send a verification SMS, select the correct SIM and allow the app to complete that step.
  3. Check the result: Continue only when the app confirms verification. If it fails, check network access, outgoing SMS availability and the bank or app's support instructions.

How to Link Your Bank Account

After the required verification, a supported app may display eligible bank accounts associated with the registered mobile number. Which accounts appear depends on the bank, account eligibility and the app's current support.

  1. Navigate Menu: Go to the Profile or Payment Methods menu inside your UPI application and tap Add Bank Account.
  2. Select Institution: Choose your banking institution from the list of supported entities.
  3. Choose an eligible account: Select the correct account from the list made available by the app and follow the bank's confirmation steps. Not every account type or bank is necessarily supported.
  4. Choose Account: Select the primary savings or current account you wish to attach to your profile to generate your unique UPI ID (Virtual Payment Address).

Which Bank Accounts Can Be Linked to UPI?

Eligible savings and current accounts are commonly supported, but the available account types depend on the issuing bank and UPI app. Some overdraft accounts may also be eligible. Certain RuPay credit cards and prepaid payment instruments can be linked through supported issuer and app arrangements, subject to eligibility and transaction restrictions.

Do not assume that every bank account, credit card or wallet can be linked. Check the current instructions from your card issuer, prepaid instrument issuer or bank before attempting setup.

How to Link Multiple Bank Accounts

Many UPI applications let users link more than one eligible bank account. Availability and the number of accounts supported depend on the bank and app.

  1. Access Settings: Open Profile Settings and select Add Another Bank Account.
  2. Fetch Details: Choose the second financial institution where your mobile number is registered and let the system fetch the details.
  3. Choose a preferred account: Where the app offers this option, select the account you want to use for outgoing payments. Incoming payments are credited according to the UPI ID or number and its account mapping; a preferred debit account does not necessarily change where incoming funds are received.

How to Set Your UPI PIN for the First Time

A UPI PIN is a confidential authentication code used to authorize applicable payments. Follow the PIN length and setup steps shown by your bank and official app.

  1. Initiate Setup: Access your linked bank account inside the app and tap Set UPI PIN.
  2. Choose a supported method: Select one of the verification options currently offered by your bank and app. Depending on provider support, this may include debit-card details, Aadhaar-based verification or another approved method.
  3. Verify OTP: Verify the auto-generated One-Time Password (OTP) dispatched to your registered mobile number.
  4. Create PIN: Set your custom 4-digit or 6-digit PIN and re-enter it to confirm establishment.

How to Reset a Forgotten UPI PIN

If you forget your UPI PIN, use the reset option provided in your official payment app. The required verification method and whether additional bank support is needed depend on the issuer and app.

  1. Select Reset: Open Payment Settings, locate the specific bank account, and select Forgot PIN or Reset PIN.
  2. Identity Verification: Complete the verification method currently offered by your bank and app. Never read an OTP to another person or enter it through a link or screen shared by an unsolicited caller.
  3. Confirm New PIN: Upon successfully validating the security code, create and confirm your new UPI PIN.

Setting a UPI PIN Using Debit Card Details

  1. Select Option: Select the Debit Card option during PIN setup.
  2. Enter Details: Input the last 6 digits of your card along with the expiry date (MM/YY).
  3. Bank OTP: Enter the dual-verification Bank OTP sent via SMS.
  4. Finalize PIN: Key in your new UPI PIN twice to complete configuration.

Setting a UPI PIN Using Aadhaar Verification

  1. Select Aadhaar Option: Select Aadhaar-based Verification during the PIN creation workflow.
  2. Enter Digits: Enter the first 6 digits of your Aadhaar card.
  3. Dual OTPs: The platform will issue two separate verification codes: an Aadhaar OTP from UIDAI and a Bank OTP from your financial institution.
  4. Confirm PIN: Validate both OTPs within the app, then define and confirm your new UPI PIN.

How to Activate UPI on a New Phone

When you move to a new phone, the UPI app may ask you to verify the registered mobile number or repeat its device-registration steps. The exact process varies by provider.

  1. Install & Insert SIM: Download your preferred UPI app on the new phone and insert the bank-registered SIM card into the new device.
  2. Complete any requested verification: Follow the SMS or other device checks shown by the official app.
  3. Restore account access: Confirm or relink eligible accounts if the app asks. Do not assume account access is restored automatically; use the provider's support route if it is not.

What to Do After Changing Your SIM

If you replace a SIM but keep the same number, or change the mobile number registered with your bank, the steps can differ. A number change generally requires updating the bank's customer records and then following the UPI app's re-verification instructions.

  1. Update at Bank: Update your official mobile number first through your bank’s net banking portal, mobile app, or branch.
  2. Re-register: Launch your UPI app and initiate a fresh registration process using the new SIM card.
  3. Fetch Accounts: Re-verify the device and fetch your bank accounts linked under the new mobile identity.

What Happens When You Reinstall a UPI App?

Reinstalling a UPI app removes its local app data and may require you to register or verify the device again. Follow the current prompts to restore account access; the app may ask you to select or relink an eligible account.

Do not assume that every setting, mandate or wallet balance is automatically restored after reinstalling or changing a device. Check each feature separately in the official app and contact your bank or provider if an item is missing.

How to Restore Access to UPI After Changing Phones

  1. Install the official payment app from its authorized app store or the bank's official website.
  2. Register with the mobile number associated with your bank account and complete the verification steps shown by the app.
  3. Confirm which eligible bank accounts are available and review your UPI IDs, mandates and balances before making a payment.
  4. Check the transaction status in the app after any transfer. Contact the bank if access or account details do not appear as expected.

How to Set Up UPI LITE on a New Device

UPI Lite is an on-device balance feature for eligible small-value payments. Availability, funding and payment flows depend on the bank and UPI app. The RBI's 4 December 2024 notification sets a maximum balance of ₹5,000 and a per-transaction limit of ₹1,000 under the offline small-value payment framework. Providers may apply eligibility checks or more restrictive controls, and rules can change. See the RBI notification on the enhanced UPI Lite limits.

  1. Open the UPI Lite section in a supported official app and review its eligibility and terms.
  2. Select an eligible linked account and follow the in-app process to add funds. The app may ask for your UPI PIN to authorize the load.
  3. Before making a payment, check the amount and recipient in the app. Eligible UPI Lite payments may not require a UPI PIN within the supported limits.
  4. Before changing devices, check the app's UPI Lite closure or transfer instructions. Do not assume the balance automatically moves to another phone.

Practical checks before you act

UPI setup requirements depend on the app, bank and supported authentication path.

  • Install the official app and use the mobile number registered with your bank.
  • Complete account linking only inside the official application.
  • Set or reset the UPI PIN only through the supported bank/app flow.

For the current registration process, check the official NPCI UPI FAQs and your bank's published instructions. NPCI's October 2025 circular describes optional additional authentication methods, including UIDAI face authentication for PIN setup/reset and on-device biometrics for eligible transactions. The RBI's December 2024 notification specifies UPI Lite limits of ₹5,000 total balance and ₹1,000 per transaction. These are subject to provider implementation, customer eligibility and later changes; check the app and bank instructions.

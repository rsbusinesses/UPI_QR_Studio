UNDERSTANDING UPI

One payment system.
Multiple ways to pay.

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) lets people make payments between participating bank accounts through supported apps. You may use a third-party payment app or a bank's own application, depending on its supported services and eligibility requirements.

KNOW THE BASICS

What does a UPI ID look like?

A UPI ID, also called a Virtual Payment Address (VPA), is an identifier used to route payments through UPI. Its format and available handle can depend on the app, bank and registration.

A UPI ID is not the same as a bank account number. Do not assume that a particular suffix belongs to a provider unless you have confirmed it through that provider's official app or support.

COMMON QUESTIONS

UPI providers FAQs

Quick answers to common questions about UPI IDs and apps.

What is a UPI ID provider?

It generally refers to an app or participating service through which a user can access UPI. The bank and payment service arrangements behind an app may differ.

Can I use UPI without changing my bank?

In many cases, you can use a supported UPI app with an eligible account at a participating bank. Check your bank and app's current requirements before registering.

Does every UPI app use the same UPI ID handle?

No. Available handles can differ between apps, banks and supported configurations. Confirm your actual UPI ID in your official app rather than relying on a generic example.

Can I trust every website listing a UPI provider?

No. Use the provider's official website and verified app-store listing. Never share your PIN, OTP or banking credentials with a website or person claiming to help you.

Where can I check official UPI information?

Start with the National Payments Corporation of India's UPI information and FAQ pages. You should also consult your bank and the payment app's official support resources.
